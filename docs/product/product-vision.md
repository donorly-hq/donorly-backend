# Product Vision

> Status: DRAFT — Zahed to review, edit, and own this document.

## Positioning

An AI-powered fundraising operations platform for mosques, nonprofits, community organizations,
and event-based fundraising teams.

## The two halves

**Trusted deterministic core** (must never be probabilistic):
- Organizations, users, roles, permissions, multi-tenant isolation
- Donors, pledges, payments, receipts
- Campaigns, events, townhalls, follow-ups, inventory
- Communications, dashboards, reporting, immutable audit history

**AI differentiation** (where Donorly wins):
- Natural-language fundraising intelligence over the org's own data
- Pledge recovery recommendations with evidence
- Draft-for-approval communications (AI drafts, humans send)
- Campaign and event performance analysis
- Approval-based workflow automation (never full autonomy until earned)

## Who it serves

It is a fundraisers centric application. It should have all the components needed from start till we recieve the money and send a thank you note and updates about how the funds were utilized.

## What success looks like

Does Donorly help an organization collect more outstanding pledges, make better fundraising
decisions, or save meaningful administrative time? Metrics tracked:
- Pledge recovery attributable to follow-ups
- Administrative hours saved
- AI draft acceptance rate
- Active organizations and weekly active users
